# Campaign & Launch Route

Use this route for product launches, integrated campaigns, campaign concepts, coordinated web/email/social/ad assets, launch narratives, and visual systems that must remain recognizable across channels.

A campaign is one strategic idea adapted to multiple contexts, not a set of unrelated attractive assets.

## Campaign contract

Define:

- business objective
- audience and moment
- launch or campaign promise
- offer and call to action
- reason to care now
- proof and constraints
- channels and formats
- campaign duration
- success signals
- legal, brand, localization, and approval constraints

Separate the durable product position from the temporary campaign idea.

## Concept system

Write one sentence that explains the campaign's central idea without naming a visual effect. Then define:

- core message
- visual metaphor or world
- hero asset
- typography and composition behavior
- color roles
- motion or transition behavior
- image and illustration rules
- recurring motif
- allowed variation
- forbidden drift

The system must be recognizable when cropped, resized, localized, or stripped down to one static asset.

## Channel adaptation

For each channel, state:

- audience state
- communication job
- required content
- primary action
- format and technical constraints
- safe area and crop behavior
- animation or duration limits
- relationship to the landing experience

Do not force desktop web composition into social or email dimensions. Preserve the concept, not the exact layout.

## Asset matrix

Inventory what is real and what must be created:

- logo and lockups
- product screenshots or footage
- photography
- illustration
- diagrams
- iconography
- campaign key visual
- motion assets
- social crops
- email modules
- landing-page sections
- metadata and share images

Track provenance and usage rights. Generated assets should have a visual bible and a named anchor so the set does not drift between outputs.

## Messaging hierarchy

Maintain one promise and vocabulary across touchpoints, while adapting depth:

- ad or teaser earns attention
- social or email establishes relevance
- landing page explains and proves
- product/signup flow fulfills the promise

A campaign fails when the destination changes the claim, visual language, or expected action.

## Variants and testing

Variants must test a hypothesis, such as message angle, audience framing, proof type, or art-direction intensity. Keep the brand and offer recognizable. Do not generate five unrelated concepts and call them a campaign.

## Delivery

Provide:

1. campaign thesis
2. audience and offer
3. messaging hierarchy
4. visual system
5. channel matrix
6. asset list and provenance gaps
7. variants or adaptation rules
8. measurement plan
9. quality and approval checks

Verify text accuracy, logo use, crops, contrast, motion, localization expansion, destination consistency, and all factual claims.
