# Design contract

Use only for a new direction, a substantial redesign, or an explicit handoff.
Do not create this file automatically for a small change. Replace the bracketed
fields and delete irrelevant sections. Keep the completed contract short.

## Intent and scope

**Audience / job:** [Who is doing what?]
**Primary action:** [One meaningful next action, or the task the interface supports.]
**Deliverable:** [Page, component, flow, review, or exploration.]
**Preserve:** [Existing tokens, behavior, APIs, brand, and content that must not change.]
**Known facts / open questions:** [Separate evidence from assumptions; no fabricated proof.]

## Direction and ownership

**Primary style:** [Existing system or one selected style.]
**Rationale:** [Why it fits this content, audience, and available assets.]
**Composition:** [Focal point, reading order, image scale, density, and section rhythm.]
**Type roles:** [Display, reading, UI, metadata; actual families only when chosen.]
**Color and surfaces:** [Semantic roles, contrast expectations, shape, border, and depth.]
**Image direction:** [Subject, crop, light, palette, provenance, and fallback.]
**Signature:** [Zero or one high-salience treatment by default.]
**Explicit exclusions:** [Two or three plausible treatments that would dilute the direction.]

For an intentional hybrid, name which system owns typography, color, geometry,
imagery, and motion. Bound the secondary influence to a region or role. Otherwise
omit the hybrid discussion entirely.

## Behavior and constraints

**Real states:** [Pending, empty, error, success, selection, open/closed as relevant.]
**Responsive changes:** [What reflows, collapses, crops, or becomes a normal list.]
**Motion:** [Purpose, timing family, interruption, reduced-motion behavior.]
**Fallbacks:** [Missing media/font, unavailable feature, blocked autoplay, enhancement failure.]
**Implementation:** [Existing stack and primitives; any proposed dependency with justification.]
**Performance budget:** [Agreed constraints and the measurement plan; do not invent universal limits.]

## Evidence and acceptance

**First finished slice:** [A representative area that validates the visual system.]
**Checks:** [Actual widths, states, keyboard path, preferences, and project commands.]
**Content approval:** [Sources for claims, metrics, logos, testimonials, and media rights.]
**Completion evidence:** [What must be observed, not just asserted.]
