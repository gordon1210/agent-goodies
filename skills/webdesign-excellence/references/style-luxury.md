# Style: quiet luxury and material confidence

**Load when:** the selected direction depends on craft, hospitality, premium objects, or calm confidence.

## Design thesis

Convey quality through material evidence, pacing, and detail. Luxury is not the
combination of black, gold, thin text, and an oversized logo. A restrained page must
have something worth looking at and a clear reason for its spaciousness.

## Grammar to establish

Choose one expressive type voice and a highly readable supporting voice. Tune
contrast in weight and scale rather than relying on extremely thin strokes. Pick
an intentionally warm or cool neutral system and a controlled accent. Reserve
metallic or foil cues for imagery or a tiny graphic detail; avoid simulated gold
on every control.

Use generous but purposeful intervals. A product detail can occupy a large region
if it reveals material, finish, construction, or context. Keep image lighting,
white balance, perspective, and crop coherent. Captions and specifications should
be easy to find, not disguised as decorative microtype.

## Concrete composition

For a crafted object: one confident product image, a short statement of value,
material or making detail, specifications, and an unambiguous purchase or inquiry
path. For hospitality: atmosphere, location context, room evidence, practical
information, and booking. Brand storytelling must not hide the decision facts.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

A quiet image reveal, measured page transition, or restrained image treatment may
support the pace. Actual controls still respond promptly. A slow image pan is not
permission to delay form feedback. Use translucency only when the navigation really
overlays media and the legibility strategy is stable.

**Avoid:** tiny beige-on-cream copy; excessive tracking on long body text; unexplained
full-screen introductory sequences; loading rituals; content hidden behind hover;
stock imagery with incompatible lighting; grand copy without verifiable substance.
Do not mix neon terminal decoration or hard comic-style shadows into this direction.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Spacious desktop art direction should become a carefully edited mobile sequence,
not several screens of empty margins. Crop to preserve the subject. Show price,
policy, availability, or inquiry expectations where needed. Keep text size and
control targets practical even when the visual tone is delicate.

**Pass:** perceived quality comes from coherent assets and exact details; the
experience remains clear, readable, and actionable without a decorative overlay.
