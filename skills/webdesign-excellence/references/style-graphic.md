# Style: expressive graphic and chromatic

**Load when:** the chosen direction is bold, cultural, playful, or campaign-led.

## Design thesis

Create energy through controlled graphic relationships: color fields, bold type,
illustration, scale, and rhythm. Expressive does not mean every region competes
for attention, nor does it require a single neutral palette.

## Grammar to establish

Choose a limited set of approved color pairings, each with a clear foreground and
background role. Multiple accents can be valid, but text contrast and state meaning
must remain predictable. Pick one dominant geometric device: blocks, bands,
stickers, cutouts, or repeated symbols. Make its placement obey a layout rule.

Use strong display typography and a restrained reading layer. Avoid turning every
label into a poster. Let type occupy actual space rather than floating arbitrarily
over photography. Select a coherent illustration language and keep stroke, grain,
and lighting aligned across assets.

## Concrete composition

An event page can open with a typographic identity block, place date and location
where they are immediately useful, then transition to program, participants, and
registration. A creative campaign might instead use a giant cropped asset beside
a headline. In both cases, practical information must not depend on deciphering
the visual concept.

Create rhythm with a few strong graphic changes and quieter intervals. Equal
cards are appropriate for a genuinely comparable schedule; not every tile needs
a unique rotation, border, or radius.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

A single kinetic heading, a decisive color wipe, or a scrollable artwork sequence
can be the signature. Use motion to amplify an already legible graphic concept.
A constant marquee is a separate interaction commitment, not free decoration.

**Avoid:** unreadable type collisions; all sections in unrelated accent colors;
multiple concurrent tickers; essential text embedded only in a poster image;
comic-style shadows added without a graphic rationale. Do not flatten every
expressive request into technical monochrome because it is easier to standardize.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Recompose the mobile poster. Keep key event or offer details in normal readable
flow. Preserve text alternatives for illustration and a non-moving version of
kinetic information. Test every color pairing, not just the primary brand colors.

**Pass:** the page is unmistakably expressive but still has a dominant message,
a calm information layer, and a reliable action path.
