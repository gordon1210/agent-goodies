# Style: deliberate neo-brutalist clarity

**Load when:** a raw, direct, assertive visual language is explicitly selected.

## Design thesis

Make the construction visible and the communication direct. This is a deliberate
graphic style, not a justification for broken layout, missing states, or poor
accessibility. Its modernity depends on execution and fit, not thick borders alone.

## Grammar to establish

Choose a border weight family, one hard-offset depth model, and a restrained set
of strong color pairings. Keep the geometry intentional: square or simple rounded
forms, direct alignment, clear content hierarchy. Type can be large and forceful;
body text remains comfortable to read.

An offset shadow is a graphic plane, not a realistic light source. Keep its direction
and distance consistent. If press feedback moves the visible plane toward the
shadow, retain a stable interaction hit area. Make focus unmistakable without
confusing it with the permanent heavy border.

## Concrete composition

For an independent tool or direct campaign, lead with an explicit proposition,
a practical visual, clear comparison or proof, and one primary action. Dense
information can live in structured rows. Use strong dividers to organize it rather
than wrapping every paragraph in a loud sticker.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

Flat color inversions, small press translations, a typographic reveal, or simple
horizontal artwork strips can fit. A conventional responsive grid is often enough.
Do not add soft glass, cinematic fog, and neon glow to “modernize” a coherent hard
plane system. If the user requested that contrast, define which region owns it.

**Avoid:** default browser styling presented as finished art direction; jagged
contrast everywhere; an aggressive visual tone that conflicts with the audience;
misaligned controls; giant borders reducing useful mobile space; hidden navigation
in pursuit of rawness. Preserve native semantics even when the visual style is unconventional.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Reduce graphic thickness or offset where it crowds narrow layouts. Keep controls
large enough to use and avoid horizontal clipping caused by offset planes. Ensure
long translations and error messages still fit. In forced colors, useful control
boundaries and labels must survive without the shadow.

**Pass:** the result feels authored, direct, and consistent, not unfinished or
hostile to its users. Do not prescribe this style as a universally current default.
