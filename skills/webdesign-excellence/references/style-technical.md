# Style: technical precision

**Load when:** the selected direction emphasizes structure, tooling, engineering, or credible systems.

## Design thesis

Make precision visible through alignment, information structure, and authentic product
content. This is not a requirement to make every software product resemble a terminal.
The key contrast is high structural discipline with little visual noise.

## Grammar to establish

Choose a neutral canvas, a readable ink color, and a restrained accent strategy.
Use a functional sans for general reading; reserve mono for code, measurements,
identifiers, or carefully limited metadata. Define a divider hierarchy: structural
rules, local grouping, and interactive boundaries have different jobs.

Prefer shared planes and aligned columns over independent floating containers.
Square or small-radius shapes can strengthen the grammar, but retain an existing
accessible component's sensible shape unless changing it serves the direction.
Use numerals and indices only where they help navigation or understanding.

## Concrete composition

For infrastructure software: a direct proposition aligned with a real or explicitly
illustrative system view; a short capability matrix; one workflow explained with
input, transformation, and output; then evidence and the next action. Replace
abstract orbital decoration with an actual dependency, deployment, or inspection
model when that communicates the product more clearly.

A shared 1px grid can create structure without heavy card frames. Select the grid
technique module for implementation. An understated image or diagram can supply
most of the visual interest while the controls stay quiet. Use text labels to clarify
status; small colored dots alone are insufficient.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

A functional product micro-demo, inline code specimen, or diagram is often a better
signature than ambient animation. A real technical diagram need not be permanently
rotating. Do not mix glowing glass planes, embossed controls, and a paper blueprint
unless a scoped hybrid was requested.

**Avoid:** fake uptime, invented latency, meaningless coordinates, unreadable mono
paragraphs, all-caps microcopy, grids behind every table, and forced square styling
that weakens existing controls. The inspected inspiration prompt uses several such
risky details; this dossier retains its structural idea, not its entire prescription.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Collapse visual diagrams thoughtfully and provide an equivalent textual explanation.
Keep wide code and genuinely two-dimensional tables in deliberate accessible regions.
Lower decorative grid contrast before lowering text contrast. On small screens,
prioritize the actual proposition and action over the decorative terminal frame.

**Pass:** the interface communicates a real system with consistent alignment and
credible information, not merely the costume of a system.
