# Style: cinematic and narrative-led

**Load when:** moving imagery or a strong image sequence is central to the chosen story.

## Design thesis

Treat the opening as one carefully framed scene with a clear message. Cinematic
quality comes from footage, framing, light, editing, and pacing—not a video tag
behind text and a permanent loading screen.

## Grammar to establish

Choose the subject and focal region first. Define where copy can sit throughout
the shot, not only on its first frame. Use a stable scrim or content plane when
background luminance varies. Keep navigation and calls to action optically quiet
but readily visible. A sparse display voice can complement the scene; body and
controls remain practical.

Use a controlled palette drawn from the media or a deliberate contrast against it.
Let the scene provide texture and depth. Avoid adding blobs, particles, glass cards,
and a custom cursor merely because there is visual room.

## Concrete composition

For a place, product, or crafted experience: one opening frame; a concrete account
of what is being offered; a sequence of supporting details; practical evidence;
then the next action. A short film can be user-initiated rather than an autoplaying
background. The page should still communicate the offer from its poster alone.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

A controlled clip transition, a single scroll chapter, or restrained image parallax
can serve the narrative. Load video and motion guidance before implementation.
Never prevent navigation while an intro runs. Use a direct content route instead
of scroll hijacking. Sound requires explicit user control.

**Avoid:** footage that never reveals the product; illegible text over changing
highlights; HLS or WebGL added without need; unbounded full-screen blur; excessive
100vh filler; dark overlays used to conceal low-quality assets; fake progress bars.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Prepare mobile crops and a useful poster. Do not download heavy moving media solely
to reproduce a desktop atmosphere on a constrained device. Respect reduced motion
and blocked autoplay. Provide the necessary pause/stop controls, and captions or
alternatives for meaningful media. The fallback must retain the meaning of the scene.

**Pass:** the first frame already works as a design, movement deepens understanding,
and loading, low power, touch, or motion preferences never block the primary task.
