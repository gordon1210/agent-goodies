# Style: atmospheric depth

**Load when:** the selected direction uses light, color fields, or environmental depth behind a quiet interface.

## Design thesis

Put visual richness in the environment and clarity in the foreground. This differs
from glass-led design: the foreground can be entirely opaque, and the atmosphere
may be a static image or gradient rather than a stack of transparent cards.

## Grammar to establish

Choose one light or color family and define a focal region. Let the background
fade into useful quiet areas. Keep foreground text and controls on predictable
contrast. The environmental palette may be richer than the functional UI palette.
Do not assign a new glow hue to every component.

Pick a display voice with appropriate contrast against the environment and a
readable interface voice. A serif is possible, not required. Use restrained
surface levels and consistent radii. A subtle grid may contextualize a technology
product, but it is unnecessary for an emotional lifestyle story.

## Concrete composition

For an abstract software product, place a clear claim and credible product visual
against one atmospheric field. Move into quieter evidence sections when users need
to read closely. Returning to atmosphere near a closing statement can create rhythm,
but alternating dark and light sections mechanically is not a narrative.

The inspected inspiration combines a layered background with clean foreground
content; that separation is reusable. Its exact fonts, gradient colors, glass
panels, and animation timings are not a mandatory bundle.

## Optional techniques and boundaries

A static mesh-like color field, masked grid, tiny grain layer, or slow optional
ambient effect may support the direction. Begin with a static environment and
measure the value of movement. Prefer a prepared image over expensive real-time
blur when it produces the same useful result.

**Avoid:** animated blobs as filler; glowing text, inputs, cards, and borders all
at once; low-contrast muted text; nested transparent surfaces; invisible actions
until a reveal completes. This direction does not automatically justify cyberpunk
metadata or cinematic video.

## Adaptation and acceptance

Reposition the light field when layout changes so it does not compete with text.
Flatten or remove expensive layers on constrained devices. Test screenshots at
multiple animation positions and with reduced motion. A quiet solid surface is
an acceptable fallback if it keeps the identity and hierarchy intact.

**Pass:** the environment creates depth while the task interface remains calm,
readable, and fast. Atmosphere should be felt more than individually enumerated.
